**Title (30 chars max):** Circuit Garden: Vine Puzzle

**Short description (80 max):** Rotate glowing vines to power every flower. Calm, clever, fully offline.

**Full description:**

Circuit Garden is a relaxing logic puzzle about light and growing things.

Tap tiles to turn the vines. Guide each colour of energy from its source to the flowers that
match it, and leave no loose ends. When the last flower lights up, your garden blooms.

• 100 hand-picked puzzles, from gentle 4x4 sprouts to tricky 7x7 wildwoods
• Every puzzle has exactly one solution, checked by computer, so it is always solvable by logic
• Bridges, multiple colours and shapes add new twists as you go
• Earn seeds and grow your own garden with plants, decorations and three themes
• A new daily challenge every day, relaxed or against the clock
• Undo, restart and optional hints
• Colour-blind friendly: every colour also has its own shape
• Plays fully offline, no account needed
